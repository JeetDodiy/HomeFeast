HomeFeast – Homemade Tiffin & Food Service Platform
Detailed guide and project requirements for the HomeFeast – Homemade Tiffin & Food Service Platform analysis.

Context:
HomeFeast is a web-based platform that connects users (students, working professionals, elderly people) with verified home cooks and tiffin service providers offering fresh, hygienic, home-style meals on a daily, weekly, or monthly subscription basis. The platform digitizes ordering, subscription management, and service coordination.

Unified Mentor
Home Food
Problem Statement
Many people living away from home struggle to find healthy, affordable, and hygienichomemade food. Current food delivery apps focus mainly on restaurants, leading to:

• High cost of meals
• Unhealthy or repetitive food
• No long-term meal subscription options
• Limited visibility of local home cooks
• Manual coordination via calls or WhatsApp
Primary Objectives
• Digitize homemade tiffin and food service management
• Enable easy discovery of local home cooks
• Provide flexible meal subscriptions
• Improve food access for students and professionals
Secondary Objectives
• Support small home-based food businesses
• Improve transparency in menus and pricing
• Enable scalable expansion across cities
Scope of Work
In-Scope
• Web-based platform (responsive design)
• Tiffin & homemade food listings
• Subscription and order management
• Menu, pricing, and availability display
Out of Scope
• Native mobile applications
• Advanced AI-based meal recommendations
• Cloud kitchen integrations
Functional Requirements
User (Customer) Features
• User registration & login
• Browse nearby tiffin providers
• Search & filter by:
o Meal type (Veg / Non-Veg)
o Price
o Meal plan (daily/weekly/monthly)
o Cuisine
• View cook profile, menu, and ratings
• Subscribe or place order
• Track subscription & order history
Home Cook / Tiffin Provider Features
• Dashboard to manage profile
• Add/edit menu items
• Set pricing and meal plans
• Define service area and delivery timings
• Accept or reject subscription requests
• View orders and earnings
Admin Feature
• Manage users and cooks
• Approve cook registrations
• Monitor orders and subscriptions
• Manage categories and cuisines
• Handle complaints and disputes
Non-Functional Requirements
• Performance:
- Page load under 3 seconds
• Security:
- Secure login, encrypted user data
• Usability:
- Simple, clean, mobile-first UI
• Scalability:
- Supports multi-city onboarding

APIs
